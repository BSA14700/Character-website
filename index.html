DESIGN INTENT: This unit blends feudal aesthetics with industrial robotics. The primary challenge was maintaining the silhouette of the armor plating while ensuring the rigging (particularly the shoulder pauldrons) did not clip during combat animations. Hard-surface details were modeled in Maya, while organic wear and battle damage were sculpted in ZBrush.
